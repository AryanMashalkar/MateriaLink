    How it works

    The Greek text is split into chapters. A language model reads each description and proposes species with probabilities, and lists every use. Names are matched to NCBI taxa; compounds come from LOTUS and COCONUT; each use is checked against PubMed co-mentions and five other medical traditions.

    What it does not claim

    A paper that mentions a plant and a disease together is a signal, not proof that the plant works. Some identifications are disputed, and some plants were studied because of their reputation. Nothing here is medical advice. Hemlock, aconite and mandrake are lethal.
